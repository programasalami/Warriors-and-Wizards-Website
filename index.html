Warriors & Wizards

Warriors & Wizards is a MMORPG bullet-hell that takes place in the medieval age, where you take on the role of Warriors & Wizards and join the everlasting battle in the name of protecting the kingdom.

Play in browser Download for Windows

The Windows client runs smoother on older PCs.

What you need to play

Browser

  • Any current browser with WebGL2: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari 15+.
  • Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS. A phone or tablet loads it too.
  • About 20 MB downloaded once, then it starts fast.

Windows

  • 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
  • A graphics card or chip with OpenGL 4.3: anything from about 2013 on, including Intel HD 4400 laptop graphics.
  • 4 GB of RAM and about 150 MB of disk space.

Linux

  • 64-bit Linux with an X11 or XWayland session.
  • OpenGL 4.3 through Mesa (Intel, AMD) or the NVIDIA driver.
  • 4 GB of RAM and about 150 MB of disk space. Nothing to install.

If your machine turns on, it can probably run Warriors & Wizards.
