Warriors & Wizards

Coming Soon

A world of warriors and a whirl of wizards combine their strength and magic to take on the beasts of the Nexus, an abandoned island once accompanied by the civilizations most fiercest soldiers, until the beasts outnumbered them and overrun the place. The wizards and warriors were originally on opposing sides of the Nexus, and when it was overran by the beasts, they formed an alliance to spend the rest of their days wiping out the beasts. They started by combining their resources and building a new base together, using the wizards magic for forcefields and shields, and the warriors strength for walls and structures, they had a pretty decent base in no time. All that was left was that the wizards had to make a portal to teleport to the Nexus with a way back in case of emergency, so they did. Legend has it those beasts still linger around and carry their slain victims equipment and resources...
